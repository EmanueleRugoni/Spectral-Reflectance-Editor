Spectral Reflectance (400nm - 700nm)

Draw with the mouse (or finger) to modify the curve. You can also drop a CSV file (wavelength, reflectance) onto the chart. Note: blues near 400nm often appear slightly violet due to sRGB limitations.

Illuminant Emission Spectra (400nm - 700nm)

Click the chips to overlay sources. x, y and CCT use the observer of the selected illuminant (-02 = 2°, -10 = 10°). Fluorescent and LED spectra are parametric approximations (phosphor bands + Hg lines) fitted to the nominal chromaticity of each source; D, A and Horizon are computed from the CIE definitions. Import a measured SPD (wavelength, power) to use exact data. “SPL” entries share the spectrum of their base source.
